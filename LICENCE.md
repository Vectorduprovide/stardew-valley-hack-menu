## 🧩 About

**Stardew Valley overlay** is a curated list of Stardew Valley open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, inventory viewer, time-of-day display
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — item catalog, crop planner, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — farm layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Farm Helpers
- Time of Day Overlay — keep an eye on the in-game clock
- Inventory Viewer — organized grid of carried items
- Crop Planner Notes — seasonal planting reminders
- Movement Speed Notes — study farm traversal pacing

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Item Catalog — browse crops, seeds, tools, and furniture
- Custom Skin Notes — organize character cosmetics
- Sprinkler Layout Notes — plan efficient field coverage

### 🏋️ Social & Layout
- NPC Schedule Notes — track villager routines
- Friendship Tracker — view relationship progress notes
- Fishing Minigame Notes — timing references for study
